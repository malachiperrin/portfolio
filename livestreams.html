About this project

All of my assets start as messy scribbles lol. Here's how the Summer pack took shape.

Sketches & Brain Storming

Brainstorming Frames

This pack leans into the name: Summer. Warm greens, tropical leaves, and hand drawn flowers carry the theme across every element. The sketches here set the layout for the whole stream. A leafy frame for the face cam, a simple lower third for intros, and a chat box wrapped in flowers so the community feels part of the scenery. Nothing fancy yet. The goal at this stage was to nail the vibe and placement before making the final art.

First Iteration of these assets

Version one, assembled. The sketches became real assets: the palm leaf face cam frame in the top left, a "Follow Summer" lower third in the bottom left, and the flower framed chat in the bottom right. To test it, I placed everything over a real browser session. Overlays have to work on top of real content, not just a clean canvas. Still early days, but the foundation is there.
